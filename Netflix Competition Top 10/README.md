## Introduction
Netflix is one of the most popular streaming platforms that offers various types of shows around the globe. The dataset contains data on Netflix's globa top 10 lists from July 2021 to December 2022 to analyse the viewership trends, compare the performance of different content catgories and identify the shows with most sustained popularity over time. 

In this study, I used SQL to process the data while using tableau for data visualization.
